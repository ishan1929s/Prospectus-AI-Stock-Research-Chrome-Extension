Item 1. Business Overview

Northwind Materials Co. is a leading global developer and manufacturer of high-performance polymer coatings, resin formulations, and specialized composite materials. For the fiscal year ended August 1, 2026, total consolidated revenue grew 6% year over year to $1.42 billion, driven by accelerated adoption in our aerospace coatings division, while consolidated gross margins remained flat at 38.4%.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business, operational stability, and cash flows are subject to substantial operational, macroeconomic, and geopolitical risks. In addition to general industrial demand cycles and commodity pricing fluctuations, we face significant single-source supplier concentration risks for specialty precursor chemicals sourced from two facilities in Southeast Asia. Any logistical blockage, export restriction, or trade dispute affecting these suppliers could halt coatings production across our primary North American manufacturing facilities.

Management has increased full-year capital expenditure guidance by $45 million to fund construction of a second redundant processing plant in Texas to support expanded aerospace contracts and mitigate supply chain vulnerabilities.

Furthermore, environmental and decarbonization compliance regulations continue to evolve across our European manufacturing hubs, representing an ongoing operational expenditure and compliance overhead.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition

Operating income reached $218 million compared to $205 million in the preceding fiscal year. Deferred revenue balances stood at $64.2 million as customer pre-payments for multi-year aerospace coating deliveries expanded. Cash provided by operating activities totaled $182 million.
