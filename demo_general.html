Traditional Italian Sourdough Bread Recipe & Techniques

Baking artisan sourdough bread at home requires only three fundamental ingredients: unbleached flour, fresh water, and wild yeast starter culture. The secret to an open crumb lies in gentle bulk fermentation and proper hydration.

Fermentation and Proofing

Maintain your sourdough culture at room temperature with equal weights of whole wheat flour and filtered water. Allow the dough to ferment slowly for 12 to 18 hours to develop rich organic acids and complex flavors.

Baking in a Dutch Oven

Preheat a heavy cast-iron Dutch oven to 450°F (230°C). Steam trapped inside the sealed pot keeps the dough moist, allowing maximum oven spring before the crispy blistered crust forms.
