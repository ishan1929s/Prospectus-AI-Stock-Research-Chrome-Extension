BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY INC.

To the Shareholders of Berkshire Hathaway Inc.:

Operating earnings in 2025 reached $37.4 billion, representing a 21% increase over the previous year. Our insurance underwriting profit surged to $5.4 billion, driven by disciplined pricing and low catastrophe losses at GEICO.

Cash and U.S. Treasury bill holdings totaled $167.6 billion at year-end, providing unmatched balance sheet resilience and ample liquidity to deploy into high-return operating acquisitions.

Capital expenditures across BNSF Railroad and Berkshire Hathaway Energy totaled $14.2 billion, maintaining critical infrastructure and expanding our clean energy generation capacity.
